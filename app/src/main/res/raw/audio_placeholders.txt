Background audio files:

- tide_soft.mp3 is bundled as the default tide/ocean background.
- ocean_waves.mp3
- rain_soft.mp3
- stream_soft.mp3

The app checks raw resources by name at runtime. If an MP3 is missing, the app
continues without background audio instead of failing to compile.

Guided voice files:

To avoid a machine TTS voice, add local recordings named:

- cue_001.mp3
- cue_002.mp3
- ...
- cue_032.mp3

The app plays a cue recording first. If that recording is missing, it falls back
to Android TextToSpeech for that cue.
